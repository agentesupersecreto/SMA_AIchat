Consent, Thaw & Auto-Movement

How a female model goes from refusing an act, to allowing it, to performing it autonomously. Three interlocking systems — consent (the gate), DesHielo/"thaw" + corruption (acclimation & override), and Deseos/desire (the drive) — feed the AutoSex controller that makes her move on her own. All values verified against the SMANew rebuild source.

One-sentence model. Auto-movement requires, from her side, (1) consent for that act, (2) ongoing penetration that isn't anatomically too tight, and (3) desire above her per-zone threshold — with personality deciding how high desire must climb and stamina governing vigour. Consent is raised by thaw (slow) or bribery (instant); desire is raised by pleasurable stimulation of the matching erogenous zone.

1The big picture

Four subsystems chain together. Consent decides whether an act is even permitted; DesHielo and corruption move that gate; desire is the moment-to-moment drive; and the AutoSex controller reads consent + desire + personality every frame to decide whether she assists or takes over.

2Consent — the hard gate

Consent is checked per (stimulus type × direction × body zone × stimulating part) via ConsentNecesario.EsConsentidoConJerarquia(...), which returns an offsetMod. An act is consented when offsetMod ≥ 1.0. The default allowed/refused set lives in the static ConsensualTree.cs.

Consent is a hard prerequisite for auto-movement: in AutoSexScore.cs / AutoSexPosibilidadScore.cs, if offsetModTactil or offsetModPenetracion is below 1, the score is forced negative (Lerp(-1, maxScoreAtNoConsent=-0.333, …)) → she evades, never assists. No amount of desire overrides missing consent.

3DesHielo (thaw) & its gain

DesHielo.cs is the gradual acclimation meter. For each stimulus key it holds a 0–100 value; repeated exposure raises it, and it does not passively decay (UpdateValue adds no drift toward zero). Once an act hits 100 its further generation is throttled ×0.1, and when overall value.total ≥ 100 the limited channel zeroes (DesHieloPorCambioDeEmociones.cs:363).

Per-frame thaw gain — GetValor (DesHieloPorCambioDeEmociones.cs:372)

valor = (timeGain + arousalBonus) × typeMod × directionMod × hitMod
  timeGain     = 5/sec × personalityMod × (one-time? 2.5 : Δt)
  arousalBonus = stimulationGeneratedThisFrame × 0.01
  typeMod      = visual 1.0 | verbal 0.833 | tactile 0.667 | coital 0.5
  directionMod = she-receives 1.0 | she-performs (dada) 4.0
  hitMod       = "golpe": received 0.125 | given 8.0 | else 1.0
  personalityMod = desHieloGananciaPorPersonalidad × (1 / miedoGananciaPorPersonalidad)
Why it feels stuck. It is per-act (touching one zone won't thaw another), the fear-driven personalityMod can slow it dramatically, and each act caps at 100. Spreading attention dilutes; a fearful girl barely moves. Biggest natural lever: her performing the act (dada) = 4×.

4Corruption & bribery — the instant unlock

ConsentCorrupted.cs permanently flips a refused act to consented: CorruptEstimulo(...) adds it to m_corrupted via ConsensualTree.OverridesInvertedSet(...) and slams its DesHielo to 100 (SetTo(100f)). In gameplay this is driven by the Sobornaje / bribery dialogue (RegistroDeFuncionesDeSobornos.cs): pay money → that act is consented + fully thawed in one click. It also clears negative desire for the zone (e.g. anal bribe calls AumentarDeseoNalgas(-trasero)).

Bribe cost — CantidadNecesariaParaConversanteVaginal (≈250–2500)

num = Lerp(2500, 250, offsetMod²)                 // closer to natural consent → cheaper
num = Lerp(num,       num×0.666, gustoPorTratoExplicitoDeClientes)  // likes explicit → cheaper
num = Lerp(num×0.666, num,       gustoPorDinero)   // greedier → more expensive
num = Lerp(num×0.666, num,       1 − pobreza)      // poorer → cheaper
num = Clamp(num, 250, 2500)

Cheapest unlock = a poor, likes-explicit, low-greed girl who is already partly thawed — so grind a little first, then bribe near the 250 floor.

6How desire is raised

Stimulating a body part raises its zone's desire each frame (RegistrarEstimulo, line 690+):

gain = unidadDeAumentoZone × 100 × Δt × tier × ModEmocion × ModTipo × GainPersonalidad
       (+ 5% of every gain is banked into the ceiling — permanent, ±500 cap)
  • unidadDeAumentoZone — base unit: labios 0.00333, senos 0.0030, entrepierna 0.00267, trasero 0.00233 (≈ 0.33 / 0.30 / 0.27 / 0.23 %/sec at neutral mods).
  • tier — how central the touched part is: primario direct erogenous (clitoris/vag, mouth/tongue, anus, nipples) · segundario adjacent (labia, breasts, buttocks, perineo, lower belly) · terciario peripheral (head/ears, chest, hips/legs, belly). Each tier is itself a personality multiplier (see §10).
  • ModEmocion — pleasure (placer) ×1.0; pain ×0.75; rage ×0.5 — and pain/rage use the negative gain constant, which for most girls drains desire.
  • ModTipo — her per-personality sensitivity to the stimulus type (visual/verbal/tactile/exposure/coital).
  • GainPersonalidad — GetGainDeX() = her initialAumentoMods trait scaling.
Pleasure only. Because the negative branch is negative for almost everyone, hurting or frightening her lowers desire — and a non-consensual act generates rage, so desire can't climb until the act is consented. Consent + pleasure first; then desire builds.

7Auto-movement requirements

The engine is ControlladorDeAutoSexV2.cs, gated by the AutoSexScore / AutoSexPosibilidadScore decision layer.

Hard prerequisites (all required)

  • Consent for that act+zone (offsetModTactil & offsetModPenetracion ≥ 1), else score forced negative → evades.
  • Active penetration — stops after maxTiempoSinPenetracion = 3s without it.
  • Not "too tight" (muyEstrecho) — if penis width exceeds her tolerance, score = −1, blocked.

Graded driver + tiers

Score comes from desire via GetScorePorDeseoPercentage(desire, deseoParaSex, deseoParaRechazarSex) (−1…+1). The trait tier (§8) sets how high the score must reach:

Keeping it going

Continuation needs puedeSeguirEnAutoSex true, penetration maintained, no jam. Stamina/oxygen modulates vigour: when winded (cansancioEstado) her speed falls toward ~0.1× until she recovers (UpdateOxigeno). Speed/depth ranges come from personality (GetRangeDeVelocidadDeAutoSex).

9Fastest-path playbook

To unlock / raise consent

  1. Bribery — the only instant path: permanent consent + 100 thaw + clears negative desire. Cheapest when she's already partly thawed and is poor / likes-explicit / low-greed (≈250).
  2. Have her perform the act (dada) — 4× faster thaw than passive.
  3. Keep her aroused — arousalBonus adds to thaw on top of time gain.
  4. Avoid "golpe" on received acts (×0.125); prefer low-fear / high-thaw-gain girls.

To raise desire

  1. Stimulate the primary erogenous spot of the target zone (tier ×1, vs ×0.5 / ×0.25).
  2. Keep it pleasurable — pain/rage drain desire for most girls.
  3. Use the stimulus type she's sensitive to (her ModTipo kinks).
  4. Pick/breed muyAlto pleasure + primario + "likes this act" — stacks lower thresholds with up to ~2.5× faster gain (≈6× vs a muyBajo girl).
  5. Banking — 5% of every gain permanently raises her ceiling and survives resets, so her arousal floor creeps up across sessions.

11Persistence

  • Baseline (genetic, persistent): valoresIniciales (starting desire) and maximosIniciales (base ceiling) live in her personality/genes — part of her saved profile, and altered by AlteracionesDeDeseosIniciales / …MaxIniciales.
  • Live current desire: m_addingValores — serialized on the character instance. ResetDeseos() (line 923) zeroes it.
  • Banked ceiling bonus: m_addingMaxValores — 5% of every gain, clamped ±500. ResetDeseos() does not clear it, so the banked ceiling survives in-session resets.
  • DesHielo (thaw): per-act accumulator, no passive decay; reset only by explicit "Reset deshielos".
No explicit per-in-game-day wipe of m_addingValores/m_addingMaxValores was found; accumulated runtime state persists as long as the character instance/its serialized state does. Whether it round-trips through the day/save system was not conclusively traced.

Model Rating & Scoring System — Architecture

How the interviewer / model-picking menu's two scores — Physical Appearance ("looks") and Personality — are defined, computed, and used. The short version: neither is an objective beauty/niceness number. Both are a match score against an ideal model you designed, and the same value doubles as the fitness function of a genetic algorithm that breeds the applicant pool toward your taste. All paths under SMANew/ExportedProject/Assets/Scripts/ unless noted.

1The big idea

The rating you see is "how closely this model's genes match the ideal model you designed" — broken down per trait, scaled to 0–10. It is not "how attractive she is" in the abstract. The exact same number (fitnes, 0–1 per trait-group) is simultaneously: the slider value on screen, and the selection pressure of a genetic algorithm that evolves each campaign's applicant pool toward whatever you rate highly.

  • "Physical Appearance Score" → field aparienciaFisica — 15 sliders

3Slider ↔ value math

The sliders are a 0–10 view of an internal 0–1 fitnes. The conversion is a flat ×10 (×4 for the dropdown), in SetScore (genes → UI) and its inverse GetScore (UI → normalized dict).

DirectionLooks (15 floats)Personality floats (5)Dropdown summarizing
SetScore  genes→UIslider = fitnes * 10slider = round(fitnes * 10)enum = (PersonalidadConsenting)round(fitnes * 4)
GetScore  UI→0–1clamp(slider/10, 0, 1)clamp(slider/10, 0, 1)clamp((int)enum / 4, 0, 1)

So the dropdown maps No=0.0, Not-so-much=0.25, Some=0.5, Yes=0.75, A-lot=1.0. GetScore clamps to [0,1]; the looks SetScore does not round or clamp (the [Range] UI and ConjuntoGeneral.fitnes's own Clamp01 on set keep it bounded anyway).

Display reads the stored result, not a live recompute. The panel's TryAutoRatingAModelo() calls SetScore(sujeto.aparienciaFisica.conjuntoPorNombre) — i.e. it reads each gene-group's already-stamped fitnes. A live re-score against your profile only happens on the "Redo" action (OnRedoAutoRating → DoAutoRating).

4How fitnes is computed

AutoRatingHelper.Score(ref profile, ref interpretacion, aparienciaResult, personalidadResult) walks every gene "leaf" by reflection. For each leaf: take the integer enum-distance between your ideal and her, convert it to a 0–1 score, multiply by that leaf's weight, accumulate into a bucket keyed by the trait-group (para), then divide each bucket by its summed weights. The result is a weighted average in 0–1 — one number per trait-group.

// per trait-group `para`:
fitnes[para] = Σ( scorePerLeaf(profileLeaf, herLeaf) · weight ) / Σ( weight )

// physical leaves  → scorePerLeaf = ScoreEasy      (skinTone leaf → ScoreSkinTone)
// personality leaves → scorePerLeaf = ScoreSensible

Each leaf's para (target group) and weight come from a C# attribute on the trait — [AplicaAConjuntoDeFisico(para="skin", weigth=100)] for looks, [AplicaAConjuntoDePersonalidad(...)] for personality. The denominator (…_A_CantidadDeInterpretaciones[para]) is the sum of weights for that group, not a count — so a few heavy leaves dominate (§6). The reflection map is built once and memoized.

5The three scoring curves

The per-leaf score is a function of the integer enum-distance between your ideal and her. This is the single most important balance fact: personality uses a far stricter curve than looks.

CurveUsed fordiff 0diff 1diff 2diff 3diff ≥4
ScoreEasyall looks traits1.00.50.250.1250
ScoreSkinTonethe skin-tone leaf only1.00.750.50.250
ScoreSensibleall personality traits1.00.1 (0.11 if she overshoots)000

Consequences:

  • Looks degrade gently — being one notch off a trait still yields half credit; it takes 4+ steps to hit zero.
  • Personality is a cliff — one step off ≈ 0.1, two steps off = 0. A model can read near-zero on a personality bucket just by being two enum-steps from your ideal on its heavy leaves.
  • The "overshoot" bit (overflow) gives a hair more credit (0.11 vs 0.10) when her trait magnitude exceeds your ideal's, rather than falling short.

7Where "your taste" comes from

The profile argument to Score — the thing every model is measured against — is an ideal model you author yourself. It is not hardcoded, not a fixed set of "types," and not learned from your past picks.

  • A profile (AutoRatingProfile) is a full ideal-female gene-spec (InterpretacionCompletaDeFemale completa) + a name + a portrait. Held by the singleton SimplifiedAutoRatings.m_grupo.
  • You sculpt it in the editor PanelEditorDeAutoRatingProfiles (live 3D preview; Simple or Complete authoring modes), reached from the campaign-launch panel PanelComenzarCampaing ("New / Edit / Change profile").
  • It's saved as a portrait PNG with the genes embedded as JSON; the active profile lives in the save at root/AutoRatings/Profile/.
  • At campaign start you pick which saved profile to use; it becomes the scoring target for every model that campaign and is wiped at campaign end (RemoveProfile).
Campaign tier does NOT change your taste target. Free / amateur / professional only change how many applicants spawn and bias their genes (poverty / greed / modeling-appetite) via OverrideGenes. The yardstick stays fixed; only the population being measured changes.

8What the scores DO

When a model is rated, each category score is written onto her gene-groups as fitnes (the commit point is PiscinaDeCampaingActual.SetFemaleRate → value.fitnes = item.Value). That fitnes then drives three things:

#EffectHow
1Drives the genetic algorithm (the central mechanic)Between phases the pool "reproduces": high-fitnes models breed, low ones are culled, fitness resets. Over phases the applicant pool drifts toward whatever you rate highly. PiscinaDeNpcsManager.Reproducir / PiscinaDeSujetosReproduccionHelperV2.
2Orders who walks inWhen you don't hand-pick, the next interviewee is the highest-rated un-interviewed model, ranked looks·0.7 + personality·0.3 — the only place the two scores are weighed against each other (looks counts >2×). PiscinaDeNpcsManager…HigherRated.
3Cosmetic 0–100 dossier headlineAveraged to appearanceScore / personalityScore on the NPC dataContainer, shown (gated by dark-web subscription tier) in the dossier UI. A new-feature readout, not a mechanic.

10Personality score ≠ the live AI

A subtle but important point: the 6 personality controls are genetic gene-group buckets (conjuntos), not the fields the in-game AI uses to act. They are a rating projection over her genes.

  • The runtime AI (CuchiCuchi/AI/Personalidad.cs) works off a different representation: rasgos (0–100) + live emotions → a dominant "type" (shy / submissive / perverted / exhibitionist / rude…) via ObtenerTipoMayor / ObtenerMayorSexual, thresholded at ⅓.
  • The link is indirect: the same underlying genes both (a) aggregate into the buckets you rate, and (b) author the rasgos / traits the AI later runs on.
  • So "Servicing 8" or "Horniness 7" is a rating of her genes vs your ideal, not a stat the AI reads each frame. summarizing = "Qualified for Modeling" is your subjective guess, not a read of any InterestedInModeling flag.
There is no symbol literally named MaxTipoDePersonalidad / MAYOR_PERSONALIDAD_* in the export — the dominant-type logic is Personalidad.ObtenerTipoMayor (and ObtenerMayorSexual for the sexual type).
